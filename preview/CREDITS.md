# Demo library credits

The demo library uses open films from the Blender Foundation / Blender Studio, downloaded from Wikimedia Commons. Images were resized and recompressed to JPEG; posters marked "cropped still" are 2:3 crops of film stills. The sample clip is Commons' 480p VP9 transcode. The same list appears in the app under **Settings → Demo credits** (`src/api/demoCatalog.ts`).
